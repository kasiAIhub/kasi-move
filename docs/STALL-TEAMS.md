# Stall teams

The owner invites the first named delivery person during setup and manages Delivery team and Shop assistants under More. WhatsApp opens with a prepared invitation; the owner still taps Send. Each link expires after 24 hours, requires Accept/Decline, and can be used once. Accepted members keep a private 30-day session on that browser. Send a new invitation if they change phones or sign out. Never publish team invitation links.

Assistants can see their stall's kitchen orders, mark ready, complete collections and assign deliveries. They cannot change menus, prices, billing, settings or staff. The owner retains the money dashboard. Existing WhatsApp order alerts continue to the owner; assistants receive live updates while their kitchen is open.

New deliveries start unassigned. Owner or assistant selects an accepted available person; the order saves their name, phone and agreed fee. Only that person sees the ready job. Collection order and payment rules are unchanged. Reassignment is blocked after any item is collected. Availability affects new assignments, not existing jobs. Team removal is blocked while that delivery person has unfinished assigned jobs; otherwise access is revoked immediately. Historical fees remain intact.

The first accepted delivery person's name appears in the link-first stall introduction. Customer tracking names the actual assigned person. A speed point machine is mentioned only when delivery and card payments are enabled.

Migration 0008 adds members and invitation names/roles and carries existing accepted delivery people into the team. Tests cover permissions, invitation acceptance, assignment conflicts, availability, revocation and preserved fees.
